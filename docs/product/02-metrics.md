# Metrics & Success Criteria — Source Bible App

## North Star Metric

**Meaningful Study Depth (MSD)**

Definition:
Average number of connected study actions per session.

A connected study flow includes transitions such as:
- Verse → Cross-reference
- Verse → Word → Meaning
- Verse → Word → Usage (concordance)

Why:
The product’s core value is depth of understanding, not passive reading.  
This metric reflects whether users actually engage in structured Bible study.

## Secondary metrics

- Word Mode Adoption Rate  
  → % of users who switch to Word tab

- Verse → Word Conversion Rate  
  → % of sessions where users move from verse-level to word-level study

- Study Actions per Session  
  → number of meaningful interactions per session

- Time to First Insight  
  → time from app open to first study action

- Multi-step Study Rate  
  → % of sessions with flows like:
    - Verse → Word → Meaning
    - Verse → Cross-ref → Word

- Weekly Active Studiers (WAS)  
  → users with ≥3 study sessions per week

## Definition of success at 6 months

The product is successful if:

- ≥30% of users engage with Word mode
- ≥40% of sessions include at least one study action
- users perform multi-step study flows (verse → word → insight)
- D7 retention ≥30%
- clear evidence of repeated study behavior (not just reading)

Additionally:

- users report that the app helps them understand Scripture more deeply
- Word-level study becomes a habitual behavior
