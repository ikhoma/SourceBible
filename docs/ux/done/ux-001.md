# Observation

Swipe-to-turn only responds in the edge zones of the screen. A tester swiped
across the center (over the verse text), nothing happened, and assumed paging
was broken. On retest, paging works — it just isn't discoverable.

## Why it matters

Users naturally swipe anywhere on the page. When a center swipe does nothing,
a core reading gesture feels broken even though it works at the edges — a bad
first impression on one of the most-used interactions in the app.

## Possible direction

- Widen the swipe-active area (a wider band, or full width) — verify it doesn't
  clash with tappable original-language words, the long-press Word tab, or text
  selection (the likely reason it's edge-only today).
- Or keep edge-only and add a visible affordance: a page-edge indicator,
  chevrons, or a one-time hint so the gesture is discoverable.

## Resolution (2026-07-10)

Fixed by ADR-026 Phase 2: the reader now pages chapters in a
`UIPageViewController(.scroll)` container with a **full-surface** interactive
swipe (PDR-Page-Turn-Gesture-Zone amended → full-surface). Center swipes over
verse text turn the page; the feared clash with word tap / long-press / text
selection did not materialize — device gesture-check passed 2026-07-10 (the
PDR keeps an edge-only rollback clause just in case). Edge-only remains only
on the iOS 18 fallback path until the Phase-B target flip.
