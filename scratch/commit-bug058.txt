fix(bug-058): psalm superscriptions get their own verse_org rows (KJV/ASV/NASB)

KJV/ASV/NASB fold the psalm title into verse 1 (the eng scheme counts it as
verse 0), but verse_org tied verse 1 only to the text half: 63 titles had no
row, so tapping «sons» in KJV Ps 42:1 gave an incomplete word card and the
Original pill skipped the title. build_versification.py now writes
source='superscription' rows BEFORE the text row (targets from eng.vrs via
vrs.superscriptions; JSON loses the second title verse of Ps 60). Each row is
verified: KJV by Strong's (67/67), ASV/NASB by module form (<n> / <t>(); no
tags on the title). Goldens + completeness (67 per translation) in the script.
verse_org 155 621 -> 155 822. orgRef / verify_parallel_alignment /
build_commentary_db keep the TEXT verse as the cross-translation anchor;
4 «keep» overrides (KJV Ps 18/59/60/88) removed. ADR-028 amended.
